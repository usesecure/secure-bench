Phase 22 executes the two eligible capability-normalized lanes under the exact
corrected Phase 21 sandbox. The frozen population contains 112 OpenGrep 1.22.0
attempts and 112 Semgrep CE 1.170.0 attempts, one per opaque Phase 19 case ID,
with zero retries and no Secure Engine process.

This phase is always classified as a **post-open recovery study**. The corpus
was opened in Phase 20, so Phase 22 cannot restore one-shot or globally blind
holdout validity. Its independent ledger, denominators, raw evidence, and
metrics do not alter or replace the 224 historical Phase 20 normalized-lane
failures. Native and capability-normalized metrics remain separate, and no
overall three-scanner winner is defined.

The exact plan and execution contract are frozen before the irreversible
corpus-open marker. Preflight uses only immutable metadata and synthetic Phase
21 canaries. After opening, no synthetic scanner process is allowed. The
scanner-free verifier reconstructs results, strata, pairs, operational states,
performance summaries, paired disagreements, and the historical phase table
from raw evidence.
